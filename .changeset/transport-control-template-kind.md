Template kind (`document_kind`) in the pinned contract. The same 714 operations and 572 paths, none added or removed, and no method signature changes.

- `TenantPdfTemplate` and `PdfTemplateRevision` carry `document_kind` (`"commercial" | "transport-document"`), always present in responses and in the 2xx examples. `pdfTemplates.create` accepts it in its body (`commercial` by default) and `pdfTemplates.list` accepts the `document_kind` filter in `params`. A template never changes kind. An unknown value is a `422` `invalid_pdf_template_configuration` (`ValidationError`) with `param` `document_kind`, in create, list and publish too, and a transport control template without the transport documents module in the plan is a `403` `feature_not_available_in_plan`.
- `pdfTemplates.duplicate` takes an optional `document_kind`. Omitted, the copy keeps the kind of its source. With `transport-document` on a commercial template it creates a new control template that copies only the design, the font (8 to 11 pt), the colors and the brand. Control to commercial, or an unknown value, is a `422` `invalid_pdf_template_configuration` with `param` `document_kind`, and without the module it is a `403` `feature_not_available_in_plan`.
- `pdfTemplates.defaultsSet` only accepts a template whose kind is `transport-document` for the `transport-document` entry of `type_defaults`. A template of another kind is a `422` `invalid_pdf_template_configuration` with `param` `type_defaults.N.template_id`.
- 13 operations declare a retryable `503` (a `ServerError` with `retryAfter`) when the PDF engine does not answer (`pdf_engine_unreachable`, nothing written): `invoices.issue`, `invoices.send`, `quotes.send`, `proformas.send`, `deliveryNotes.send`, `quotes.accept`, `proformas.accept`, `invoices.substituteSimplified`, `revisePresentation` of invoices, quotes, proformas and delivery notes, and the draft preview of an invoice. The first twelve need the engine only to check the reviewed presentation or to build the revision. Its terminal failures stay the `500` `pdf_engine_profile_unavailable`.
- Descriptions of `tax_rate` and `retention_rate` in document lines: the tax can be chosen with `tax_rate_id` or `retention_rate_id`, and a rate that differs from the chosen tax is a `422` `parameter_invalid_value` with subcode `tax_rate_mismatch`.
